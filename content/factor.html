Momentum beat the Nifty 200 in 15 of the last 20 calendar years. Then, over the last five years, it trailed the plain index, while "boring" value stocks made 22.4% a year.

What each factor buys

Momentum: the 30 Nifty 200 stocks with the strongest 6- and 12-month price returns, adjusted for volatility. Rebalanced every June and December.
Value: the 30 stocks that look cheapest on earnings-to-price, book-to-price, sales-to-price and dividend yield.
Quality: the 30 stocks with high return on equity, low debt-to-equity and steady earnings growth over the previous five years.

Why the winner changes

Think of cricket. On a green pitch the fast bowler takes wickets; on a dry pitch the spinner does. The bowlers have not changed, the pitch has. Market conditions (interest rates, which sectors lead, how quickly trends reverse) are the pitch:

  • Momentum does best in long, steady trends and suffers when leadership flips quickly. In 2026 its sector mix swung from financials to capital goods, power and metals within six months.
  • Value tends to shine when beaten-down sectors recover.
  • Quality tends to hold up in slowdowns, and can lag in sharp junk rallies.

The mistake to avoid

Five years ago momentum looked unbeatable, and money poured in: by February 2026, 24 passive funds tracked NSE momentum indices with over ₹17,000 crore. Switching now to whatever won the last five years (value) is the same decision under a new name.

How to use factors sensibly

  1. Judge over a full cycle. Look at 10+ years and at the worst stretches, not the last one to three years.
  2. Know the bad season in advance. Momentum: Nifty200 Momentum 30 fell 67.7% peak to trough in 2008, against 63.7% for the Nifty 200.
  3. Consider combining factors. Factors that win at different times smooth the ride when held together.
  4. Decide your rebalancing rule now, for example once a year back to fixed weights, so a bad season doesn't make the decision for you.
  5. Check the product: tracking error and expense ratio of the index fund or ETF you use.
