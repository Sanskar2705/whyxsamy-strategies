What a 2x ETF does

It aims to deliver two times the stock's return each day. It does not promise two times the return over a month or a year.

Why ₹1.82 disappeared: the gain was 20% of ₹100 = ₹20. The loss was 18.18% of ₹120 = ₹21.82. Same percentage logic, bigger base. Every up-and-down round trip shaves a little.

The rule of thumb

Daily drag of a 2x fund ≈ (daily swing)²
e.g. ±9.5% days → ≈ 0.9% lost per day while the stock goes nowhere

The bigger the daily swings, the faster the decay. In a steady one-way trend a 2x fund can beat 2× the stock; in choppy markets it falls behind. Fees and borrowing costs come on top of this.

The real-world case

Korea · 16 June → 15 July 2026

Retail investors put a net 5.85 trillion won into single-stock 2x leveraged ETFs on two large chipmakers in one month. Over the same month the two underlying shares fell 19.5% and 24.3%. A 2x daily product falls roughly twice as hard, plus decay.

Checklist before touching a leveraged ETF

  • Do I understand that it resets daily, and my result over weeks will not be 2× the stock?
  • Am I holding it for a day or two, as designed, or for months?
  • Have I calculated the decay at today's volatility (calculator above)?
  • Can I afford a fall twice as large as the stock's, on top of decay?
  • Have I read the fund's prospectus section on daily objectives and compounding?
